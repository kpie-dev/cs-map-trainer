Metric definitions and limitations

Live rounds including overtime, excluding warmup and knife rounds. Participation is measured at freeze end; post-round events are excluded and survival is inferred from recorded deaths. ADR uses enemy HP damage per round played. HS% is headshot kills divided by enemy kills. Estimated Rating 2.0 is a community approximation, not an official HLTV rating or Rating 3.0. Missing inputs are not replaced with zeros. KAST uses a 5-second trade window; rating assists use 40 HP damage or a recorded flash assist.

Impact = 2.13 × KPR + 0.42 × APR − 0.41. Rating = 0.0073 × KAST% + 0.3591 × KPR − 0.5329 × DPR + 0.2372 × Impact + 0.0032 × ADR + 0.1587.
