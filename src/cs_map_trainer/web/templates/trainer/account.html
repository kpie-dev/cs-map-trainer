Your data

Your raw demo is deleted after the first analysis attempt. Reports, selections and any historical self-reported practice ratings stay until you delete them. Ratings are no longer used by the practice library. We do not use them to train models.

We store the SteamID for each analysis and your last used ID as a suggestion on your account. Team analyses also store an immutable roster of SteamIDs and optional names. Neither verifies profile ownership. Reports are private to the account that uploaded the demo, even if another account enters the same SteamID.

Deleting your account removes the suggestion, saved roster and player results. Minimal upload records, including the selected SteamID, remain through the current UTC month and until input cleanup succeeds. There is no SteamID lock or cross-account quota. A minimal Stripe customer reference remains to handle delayed events and payment cancellation. The payment provider retains billing records.
