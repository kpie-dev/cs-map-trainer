About locations

Locations are the last named areas reported by the game at the scenario snapshot, before the first damage, not the moment of death. Game areas may be broader than community callouts; names are read from the demo, not guessed from coordinates.

Grouping and weapon-based priority

All retained members are included. Groups require matching map revision, sides, weapon classes and reconstruction warnings, nearby positions for both players and similar aim directions. Broad callout names alone never join duels. Weapon priority: rifle/sniper vs pistol/SMG first, rifle/sniper vs rifle/sniper next, other or unknown matchups next, your own pistol/SMG last. This is intentional practice order, not measured mistakes or skill. “Full gun” and “eco” are weapon-class proxies only: money, armor and actual buys are not recorded. Only the retained scenarios are available; omitted candidates are not recovered.
