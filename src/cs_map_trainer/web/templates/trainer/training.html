Quick start

  1. Launch Yprac Hub 3070715607 and the required Yprac client.
  2. Open {{ map_label }} ({{ current.map.name }}) in Sandbox in an isolated local session with one human player.
  3. Paste one complete command line into the console. Do not use it in matchmaking or on a shared server.
  4. Practice, then submit your rating. Leave the session when finished and check your settings before playing elsewhere.

Commands kick existing bots and change your team, position, aim, weapon and session settings. Previous values are not saved. Bot indices, spawn readiness, eye direction, weapons and map compatibility still need in-game verification. Exporting commands does not confirm they work in game.
