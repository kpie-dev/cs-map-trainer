Where can I find my SteamID?

You need SteamID64: a 17-digit number, not your nickname, login name, or a custom profile URL.

  1. Open Steam and sign in there. Select your account name in the top-right corner, then Account details.
  2. On Account details, find Steam ID below the account heading and copy all 17 digits.
  3. Return here and paste the number into Player SteamID64. Check that this player participated in your demo.

These are simplified illustrations, not screenshots. Names and the example ID are fictional; Steam's appearance may vary. Never share your password, Steam Guard codes, or API key. We do not need them.

The ID only selects a player in your demo; it does not verify ownership or give anyone access to your reports. An attempt with a player absent from the demo counts toward your monthly limit.
